Fallback · Vocal separation

When a segment is unreadable (music, singing), the vocals are isolated and transcribed again. It runs only for those segments, so ordinary speech keeps its low latency.

UVR MDX-Net · ONNX Runtime · DirectML / CoreML / CPU
Typical latency (RX 9070 XT, balanced mode)
Final caption ≈ 0.6 s after the speaker stops · average transcribe + translate 223 ms · partial captions while you speak.
Models: Whisper large-v3-turbo (q5_0) or small, TranslateGemma 4B / 12B (Q4_K_M), Silero VAD v5, UVR-MDX-NET Voc_FT. Quality / speed is adjustable in Settings.
